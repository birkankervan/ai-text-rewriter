How it works

Three steps, no new tab.

  1. Select text

    In a text box or editor you get a Rewrite button. On regular page text you get a Translate button.

  2. Click the button

    A small panel opens next to your selection.

  3. Use the result

    Pick a tone or language, then copy the result or replace the selected text in the field with one click.

Privacy

Nothing leaves until you click.

No backend, no account, no analytics. The extension talks directly to the service you chose.

Selecting sends nothing

A request starts only after you click the Translate button, Generate, or Translate in the popup.

No server in between

Rewrites and AI translations go to your chosen AI provider with your own key. Free translation sends the selected text to Google Translate.

Keys stay on this device

API keys and settings are stored locally and never synced to your Google account.

History expires in 15 days

History stays in your browser and is deleted automatically after 15 days, or whenever you clear it.
