Why a short walk in the park lowers stress

By Elena Marsh · 6 min read

A year-long study across four cities set out to measure something most of us suspect already: time outdoors helps. The surprise was how little of it seemed to matter.

The effect held across age groups and neighbourhoods, and it was strongest for people who left their phones in their pockets. Participants who simply sat on a bench saw nearly the same benefit as those who walked.

In the end, researchers found that people who spent twenty minutes a day in a park reported lower stress within just two weeks, whether or not they exercised there.

City planners say the findings make a practical case for small green spaces close to where people work, rather than a few large parks at the edge of town.
